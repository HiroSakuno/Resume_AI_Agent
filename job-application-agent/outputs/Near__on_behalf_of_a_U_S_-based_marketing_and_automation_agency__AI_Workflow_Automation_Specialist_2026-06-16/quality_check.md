## 5. Top 3 Improvements

**1. Add "Make" and "Zapier" explicitly to the Skills section — even as "familiar with" or "learning"**
The ATS report flags "Make" as partial and "Zapier" as absent entirely. Both are listed in `required_skills`. A single line in the AI & Automation block — e.g., *"Make, Zapier (familiar; actively building)"* — closes the ATS gap and preempts an automatic filter rejection. Leaving them out entirely is a costlier signal than a qualified mention.

**2. Add a CRM or lead management context to at least one bullet — even loosely**
The word "CRM" does not appear anywhere in the resume. "Lead management," "marketing automation," and "sales pipeline" are also absent. The Dato Tecnologia role (50+ accounting clients, multi-client workflows) is the natural anchor — reframe one bullet to reference client onboarding automation, pipeline reporting, or account-level workflow standardization. This bridges toward CRM domain language without fabricating experience.
> *Current:* "Improved report consistency, scalability, and delivery efficiency across 50+ accounting clients..."
> *Revised anchor:* "...by building reusable onboarding automation workflows and parameterized data models that standardized client setup and reduced per-account implementation time — functioning as a repeatable delivery system across 50+ clients."

**3. Quantify the AB InBev migration acceleration with a time or volume metric**
The first AB InBev bullet describes the highest-value achievement in the resume but lacks a concrete output metric. "Accelerated" and "enabling AI-assisted workflow automation" are directionally strong but not anchored. Even an approximation strengthens it:
> Add: *"...reducing estimated manual analysis effort from [X weeks] to [Y days] across [N] reports"* — or reference the number of reports/metadata files processed if that's documented.

## 6. Red Flags

**⚠️ Platform Gap is Material, Not Minor**
GoHighLevel is listed as a *required* skill, not preferred. It appears in the job qualifications as "strong daily experience." Hiro has zero stated exposure. The cover letter addresses this gracefully, but an ATS pre-screen or a first-pass recruiter review could filter this out before the letter is read. This is the single largest risk in the package.

**⚠️ No Marketing or Agency Domain Signal**
The target company is a *marketing and automation agency* serving SMB clients. The resume contains no marketing, lead gen, sales pipeline, or agency context. Every domain listed (financial services, social impact, accounting, apparel manufacturing) is defensible but none maps to the agency's client base. This won't cause rejection alone, but combined with the tooling gap, it reinforces an "adjacent but not core" profile.

**⚠️ Sep 2025 Start Date on Current Role**
The AB InBev role lists "Sep 2025 – Present" on a document dated July 14, 2025. This is a future-dated start that will read as an error or inconsistency to any recruiter who notices it. Correct this immediately — it either reflects a typo (likely Sep 2024) or needs a clarifying note. This is the kind of detail that signals sloppiness at exactly the wrong moment.

## 7. Final Recommendation

### `MINOR_REVISIONS`

The application is submission-ready in structure and quality, but three targeted fixes — adding Make/Zapier to Skills, injecting CRM-adjacent language into the Dato bullet, and correcting the AB InBev start date — would meaningfully improve ATS pass-through and recruiter confidence without requiring a full rewrite. The cover letter should be submitted as-is. The core candidacy is viable; the gaps are real but not disqualifying if the resume closes the keyword distance.
