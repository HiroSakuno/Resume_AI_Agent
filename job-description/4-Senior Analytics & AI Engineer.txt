Position overview

We are seeking a Senior Analytics & AI Engineer to help optimize and evolve enterprise analytics solutions built on Power BI, SQL Server, and modern data platforms while enabling AI-driven analytics experiences using Azure AI technologies.

The role combines strong expertise in semantic models, data engineering, reporting performance, and analytics architecture with practical experience building AI-powered solutions such as copilots, AI agents, Text-to-SQL, and conversational analytics.

The ideal candidate is equally comfortable optimizing Power BI semantic models, designing scalable analytical datasets, and integrating AI capabilities on top of enterprise analytics platforms.
Responsibilities

Design and optimize Power BI semantic models for large-scale analytical workloads.
Improve report performance through DirectQuery optimization, aggregations, caching strategies, and semantic model tuning.
Develop and maintain dimensional models, analytical datasets, and data integration pipelines.
Support AI-driven analytics initiatives by exposing business metadata, semantic definitions, and governed datasets.
Design and implement AI-powered analytics solutions using Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Foundry.
Build Text-to-SQL and Text-to-DAX capabilities over enterprise analytics platforms.
Develop grounding, metadata enrichment, and retrieval strategies for AI Agents.
Implement observability, evaluation, and guardrail mechanisms for AI solutions.
Collaborate with architects and business stakeholders to define future-state analytics and AI architectures.
