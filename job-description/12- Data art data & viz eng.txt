Data & Visualization Engineer with Snowflake, Analytics Platform

Technology stack
Snowflake, SQL, Power BI, Looker, Tableau, Copilot, GenAI tools, dbt, SSIS, SSRS, DAX, Power Query, PBIP
Project overview
This role supports an analytics focused environment where data models, semantic views, and dashboards are designed to provide a consistent source of truth for stakeholders. The work includes transforming business requirements into analytical solutions, improving reporting performance, and contributing to data governance.
Position overview
We are looking for a Data & Visualization Engineer with experience in Snowflake, SQL, data modeling, and BI tools. You will be involved in building data models, developing dashboards, supporting stakeholders, and improving analytics workflows across the organization.
Responsibilities

Design and build data models connecting the Snowflake data warehouse to downstream analytics layers
Develop and optimize data transformation logic, including views, schemas, and staging areas
Define business logic and calculations to support a single source of truth
Document data lineage and relationships within data models
Design and develop dashboards with a focus on data storytelling, visual design, and user experience
Build analytics solutions using BI tools such as Power BI and Looker based on business and stakeholder needs
Troubleshoot and optimize report performance and data quality issues
Translate business requirements into analytical solutions
Collaborate with cross functional stakeholders to understand data needs
Present analytical findings and insights to leadership and operational teams
Lead proof of concept projects to test and implement analytical patterns and technologies
Contribute to data governance activities, including naming conventions, access management, and security compliance
Maintain technical documentation for data pipelines, models, and dashboard inventories
Requirements

Experience with Snowflake and advanced SQL, including query optimization, schema design, and semantic views
Understanding of dimensional modeling, data warehousing concepts, and structuring data for analytics
Experience with a modern BI tool such as Looker, Power BI, or Tableau
Experience with dashboard design principles, data storytelling, and user focused visualization
Practical experience working in AI assisted development settings using Copilot or other GenAI tools
Nice to have

Familiarity or hands on experience with dbt for transformation and modeling workflows
Familiarity with the Microsoft BI ecosystem, including SSIS and SSRS
Familiarity with Power BI specific formats such as DAX, Power Query, and PBIP
